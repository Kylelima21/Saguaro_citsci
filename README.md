# Saguaro National Park Citizen Science Explorer Display

This project was created to build a citizen science kiosk with a display for visitors to interact with and find opportunities to participate in Saguaro National Park. It is a collaborative effort between Saguaro National Park, Friends of Saguaro National Park, Western National Parks, and Schoodic Institute at Acadia National Park. 

The display page can be viewed at https://schoodic-institute-data.shinyapps.io/Saguaro_citsci/
